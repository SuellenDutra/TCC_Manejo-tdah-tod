\begin{resumo}[ABSTRACT]

    \begin{SingleSpacing}
    
       This study presents the development of a responsive \textit{web} system (TEia) designed to support the behavioral management of students diagnosed with Attention-Deficit/Hyperactivity Disorder (ADHD) and Oppositional Defiant Disorder (ODD). The primary objective is to promote integrated communication among faculty, families, and school administrators in order to align institutional monitoring strategies and reduce inconsistencies in the educational process. The research is based on the observation that the lack of effective communication and bottlenecks in incident reporting compromise the academic and social development of these students, hindering the implementation of consistent interventions. Methodologically, the study is classified as applied and qualitative research, having utilized instruments such as semi-structured interviews, \textit{online} questionnaires, and participant observation within the school community of the Federal Institute of Brasília (IFB) to accurately assess requirements. Moving beyond proposals limited to theoretical design, the project included the full implementation of the tool, coded from start to finish using native \textit{web} technologies (\textit{Vanilla JS}) and cloud infrastructure (Firebase). The system provides features for quickly recording student behavior, an instant assistant for proactive teaching strategies, institutional reports with dynamic filters, and strict profile-based access control. The consolidated results point to the optimization of school supervision, a reduction in teacher workload during times of crisis, and the strengthening of family support through a mobile timeline, reaffirming technology’s role as a fundamental bridge to inclusive education.
        
        \item\textbf{Keywords}: Web-based system. Behavioral management. ADHD. TOD. Inclusive education.
    
    \end{SingleSpacing}
    
\end{resumo}
